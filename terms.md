## 2. The service
Nerola is a game for practising English vocabulary by typing answers on an A-to-Z letter wheel. The dictionary, pronunciation audio, learning list, review words and badges are available to everyone in the free version. The first three saved new rounds have no daily limit. Afterwards the free version allows one new round per local calendar day. These three include, rather than add to, the daily round. Resuming or restarting the same round and retrying an answer do not consume another new round; an unfinished round and the learning section remain available. From version 4.0 the app also includes Nerola Coach, an AI-powered English speaking coach (see section 4).
